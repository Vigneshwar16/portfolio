Automatic Speed Control in EV’s

Sensory Evaluation Circuit Analysis DC Motor Circuit Testing

Why should a driver constantly adjust speed when the vehicle can do it smarter? I developed an intelligent EV control system that automatically adjusts motor speed based on road slope, enhancing safety, efficiency, and battery life.

• On-Road Validation – Conducted real-world tests confirming rapid response, smooth acceleration/ deceleration, and reliable hardware–app communication.

Impact: Improved driving safety by reducing the risk of overspeeding on slopes, optimized energy usage, and showcased how sensor fusion + embedded control can make EVs smarter.
